My research background is in theoretical physics, but I am also interested in active AI research, especially when it touches reasoning, scientific workflows, and mathematically structured problems. This page holds the academic side of that story.

## Current Interests

- Asymptotic symmetries of flat, AdS, and conformal gravity
- W-algebras, Carrollian conformal field theories, and celestial CFTs
- Near-boundary limits of string theory and twistor string theory on AdS
- Automated reasoning and AI for theoretical physics and mathematical problems

## Physics Background

In physics, my work has focused on asymptotic symmetries, holography, W-algebras, Carrollian and celestial conformal field theories, and nearby questions around boundary limits and structure in gravity and field theory. I still think of this body of work as a live part of my intellectual background, not just a previous chapter.
